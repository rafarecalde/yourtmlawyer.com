Filing bases

The right application for your situation

Use in commerce · Section 1(a)

For marks already in use. Requires dates of first use and a specimen showing the mark as used in the marketplace.

Intent to use · Section 1(b)

Secure a filing date before launch, then show use after the Notice of Allowance, with extensions available.

Read the guide →

Foreign registration · Section 44(e)

For owners of a qualifying foreign registration seeking U.S. registration based on that registration.
