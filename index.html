Types of Trademark Applications

We handle all types of trademark applications to meet your specific business needs.

In Use Applications

Section 1(a) Applications

For trademarks already being used in commerce. Provides immediate protection based on your first use date, requiring specimens showing actual use in the marketplace.

Intent to Use Applications

Section 1(b) Applications

Reserve your trademark before market launch. Secures your filing date while giving you up to 36 months to begin using the mark in commerce through extensions.

Madrid Protocol Applications

International Registration

For international brand owners: Leverage your existing foreign trademark registration to secure U.S. protection through a §44(e) filing basis.
