Selling on Amazon? Start With a Trademark.

Schedule a Call Our Process

June 1, 2025

Launching your product on Amazon? Before your first sale, there’s one tool that will shape your long-term success: a registered trademark. Whether you're building a custom brand or private labeling, Amazon increasingly requires sellers to verify ownership of their brand—and that starts with the USPTO.

Amazon Brand Registry: Gatekeeper to Brand Protection

Amazon’s Brand Registry program is designed to give brand owners control over their listings and the power to fight counterfeits. But enrollment requires one thing up front: a trademark that’s either registered or pending with the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO).

Once enrolled, you can:

  • Submit takedown requests for knockoffs and unauthorized resellers
  • Lock your content to prevent changes by others
  • Access tools like A+ Content and Amazon Stores
  • Use search features to monitor infringement more easily

Without a Trademark, You’re Vulnerable

Without registration, your brand can be hijacked—literally. Unauthorized sellers can use your product name, alter your listings, or even register your brand before you do. At that point, Amazon may require *you* to stop selling.

How Long Does Trademark Registration Take?

Once your application is filed, the USPTO process typically takes 8–10 months. However, Amazon allows you to enroll in Brand Registry with a pending trademark application, giving you earlier access to protection tools.
